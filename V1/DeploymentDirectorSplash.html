1. Create Your Profile

  • Create an account
  • Update your profile
  • Upload your photo (Select Camera Icon)
  • Select your skills
  • Select your resources

2. Create A Website

  • Join Rotary
  • Create your team page
  • Upload team logo (Select Upload Team Logo)(Square 1:1)
  • Upload website logo (Select Upload Website Logo)(Rectangle 16:9)
  • Set up fundraising page (Select Settings > Edit Donation Settings)

3. Build Your Team

  • Invite team members
  • Configure team administrators (Select Team Member List)
  • Activate team verified status (Select Team Member List)
  • Send updates and messages to your team (Select Team Member List)
  • Provide training (Select Training)

4. Take Action

  • Choose a deployment (Select Deployments)
  • Share deployment openings link (Select Find Open Positions)
  • Check my deployment schedule and readiness
  • View team participants
  • Track teams time
