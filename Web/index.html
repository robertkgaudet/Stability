Stability Disaster Survivor Stories



The worst thing that can happen after a natural disaster, is to be forgotten.

Stability is a story telling platform that enables you track your recovery and connects you with helpers to tell your story.

Easily share your story which can include your Amazon Wish Lists and other critical information about your current situation.

Without a Vision People Perish

At this vulnerable time in your life having a vision, visibility and simplicity are the most important things you need. By sharing and tracking your recovery with Stability, you're helping us provide community insights that accomplish all of these things.
  • Measure the remaining effort.
  • Develop a vision for recovery.
  • Build customized apps for neighbors to assist.

We're Just Getting Started

Share and tracking your recovery is the just the beginning. We're developing additional tools that allow anyone to assist neighbors in need disasters or everyday. Other concepts we're working on include.
  • Collaborative Home Rebuilding - Tools for volunteers and non-profits to manage your homes rebuilding.
  • CrowdBuying - Let donors assist you with replacings destroyed household items.
  • Volunteer Tools - Provides volunteers with publishing tools and insights into their effort.
